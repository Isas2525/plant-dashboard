Connection help

Use Chrome on Android and open this dashboard over HTTPS. Allow Bluetooth / Nearby devices when prompted. Choose SPR-PERIPHERAL. Disconnect other Bluetooth apps first. If the board is not listed, restart it and scan again.

This dashboard displays telemetry; it does not control the TFT or start recordings. Soil percentage is relative to your dry/wet calibration, not volumetric water content. Existing TFT history and SD files are not transferred.
